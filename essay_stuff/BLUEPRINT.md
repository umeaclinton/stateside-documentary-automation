# Stateside Documentary Video Essay Blueprint & Production Standard

**Channel:** Stateside  
**Handle:** @50kstateside  
**Format:** High-retention hand-drawn psychological & scientific documentary video essays.

This document locks in the exact visual, auditory, and structural specifications approved for all future documentary video essays.

---

## 1. Core Visual Aesthetic
- **Canvas:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), 1920×1080 Full HD (16:9 widescreen).
- **Linework:** Authentic hand-drawn wobbly ink outlines (thickness: 5–8px), crude/childish doodle style.
- **Character:** Minimalist stickman with round head, simple circle/dot eyes, expressive posture (slumped, shivering, upright, scratching head).
- **Stability:** **Rock-solid and 100% static.** No subpixel digital zooms or camera wobble that cause line jitter.
- **Color Palette (Functional Accents):**
  - **Black (`#000000`):** Outlines, furniture, borders, ground lines.
  - **Red (`#DC1E1E`):** Critical stats, warnings, nocebo, danger, emergency, blood pressure drops.
  - **Green (`#28B450`):** Recovery, normal vitals, placebo, health, 15-minute recovery.
  - **Blue (`#1478E6`):** Fluids, medical IV bags, calm blankets.
  - **Yellow/Orange (`#E67814`):** Question marks, fire, pills, attention highlights.

---

## 2. Pacing & Visual Frequency Rules
- **Maximum Duration Per Image:** **3.0 to 4.5 seconds.** Never let an image linger while the voiceover continues.
- **Cuts Triggered By:**
  1. Any mentioned number (e.g. *29 pills*, *6 liters*, *4 hours*, *15 minutes*, *107 men*, *15% vs 43%*).
  2. Any action or state change (swallowing, trembling, collapsing, doctor whispering, sitting up).
  3. Any conceptual shift (placebo vs nocebo, expectation vs reality).
- **Integrated Text Callouts:** Key words and numbers are drawn in bold directly into the doodle illustration to double visual retention.

---

## 4. Scriptwriting Architecture (The 5-Beat Arc)
1. **The Hook (0:00 - 0:30):** High-stakes physical drama + impossible cognitive paradox.
2. **The Conceptual Pivot (0:30 - 1:00):** Clean, jargon-free definition (e.g. Placebo = "I shall please", Nocebo = "I shall harm").
3. **The Deep Narrative Case Study (1:00 - 3:00):** Real patient/volunteer story with emotional stakes and the dramatic reveal.
4. **The Empirical Study (3:00 - 4:30):** Hard scientific experiment proving the mechanism across a large group (dates, names, percentages).
5. **Universal Viewer Application (4:30 - End):** Connects to the viewer's everyday life (2 AM searches, tight throat), ending with the signature memorable sign-off.
